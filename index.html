The Busy Beaver

"The Workhorse Wonder"
This colleague is the ultimate grinder — always first in, last out. They carry the team on their back, pick up everyone's slack, and somehow still meet every deadline. If work were a gym, they'd be deadlifting the entire department. You want them on your team… always. When chaos strikes, they don't complain — they just open another spreadsheet and get to work.
💪 Hardest Worker 📋 Ultra Reliable ⏰ Always On Time 🏆 Team MVP
🦊

The Sly Fox

"The Smooth Delegator"
Charming, clever, and suspiciously never busy. This colleague has mastered the art of pushing their work onto others with a smile. They'll compliment your skills right before asking you to "help out with a tiny thing" — which turns out to be their entire project. They take credit like a pro and dodge blame like a ninja. Beware the flattery!
🎭 Master Delegator 🗣️ Smooth Talker 🏃 Blame Dodger ⚠️ Work Pusher
🐬

The Dazzling Dolphin

"The Social Superstar"
Everyone's best friend at the office! They organize the birthday parties, remember your dog's name, and somehow know all the gossip before it happens. They may not do the most work, but they keep morale sky-high and make the office feel like a second home. The office would be a sad, grey cubicle farm without them.
🎉 Party Planner 💬 Office Glue 😊 Morale Booster 🤗 Everyone's BFF
🦉

The Watchful Owl

"The Silent Strategist"
Quiet, observant, and always three steps ahead. This colleague says little in meetings but when they speak, everyone listens. They know exactly who's doing what — and who's NOT. Watch out: they see everything, remember everything, and might just use it when the time is right. Office politics? They wrote the playbook. 👀
👀 Sees Everything 🧠 Strategic Mind 🤫 Keeps Secrets ♟️ Power Player
🐱

The Cozy Cat

"The Chill Survivor"
This colleague does the bare minimum with maximum grace. They've perfected the art of looking busy while doing absolutely nothing urgent. They'll never backstab you — that requires too much effort. They coast through chaos unbothered, somehow never get fired, and always leave on time. Honestly? Respect. We all wish we had their energy.
😌 Unbothered King/Queen 🛋️ Comfort First ⏰ Leaves On Time 🧘 Zero Stress
