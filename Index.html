🐾 Office Spirit Animal Quiz

Discover your colleague's true workplace personality!

How It Works

Answer 10 fun questions about your colleague's workplace habits.
Tally up which letter (A–E) they picked the most, then reveal their Office Spirit Animal!

🦫

The Busy Beaver

"The Workhorse Wonder"
This colleague is the ultimate grinder — always first in, last out. They carry the team on their back, pick up everyone's slack, and somehow still meet every deadline. If work were a gym, they'd be deadlifting the entire department. You want them on your team… always.

🦊

The Sly Fox

"The Smooth Delegator"
Charming, clever, and suspiciously never busy. This colleague has mastered the art of pushing their work onto others with a smile. They'll compliment your skills right before asking you to "help out with a tiny thing" — which turns out to be their entire project. Beware the flattery!

🐬

The Dazzling Dolphin

"The Social Superstar"
Everyone's best friend at the office! They organize the birthday parties, remember your dog's name, and somehow know all the gossip. They may not do the most work, but they keep morale sky-high. The office would be a sad, grey cubicle farm without them.

🦉

The Watchful Owl

"The Silent Strategist"
Quiet, observant, and always three steps ahead. This colleague says little in meetings but when they speak, everyone listens. They know exactly who's doing what — and who's NOT. Watch out: they see everything, remember everything, and might just use it when the time is right. Office politics? They wrote the playbook. 👀

🐱

The Cozy Cat

"The Chill Survivor"
This colleague does the bare minimum with maximum grace. They've perfected the art of looking busy while doing absolutely nothing urgent. They'll never backstab you — that requires too much effort. They coast through chaos unbothered, somehow never get fired, and always leave on time. Honestly? Respect.

📝 The 10 Questions

Think about your colleague and pick the answer that fits them best!

1 It's Monday morning. Where is your colleague?
2 A huge urgent project just dropped. Your colleague's reaction?
3 How does your colleague handle meetings?
4 Your colleague's email inbox looks like…
5 There's office drama. What does your colleague do?
6 How does your colleague react when someone takes credit for their work?
7 It's Friday at 4:30 PM. Your colleague is…
8 Your colleague's desk/workspace looks like…
9 How does your colleague handle feedback or criticism?
10 If your colleague were leaving the company, what would happen?

🎉 Reveal Their Spirit Animal!

Count which letter your colleague got the most (A, B, C, D, or E) and match it below!

🏆 Scoring Guide

🦫 Mostly A's → The Busy Beaver — The Workhorse Wonder. They carry the team!
🦊 Mostly B's → The Sly Fox — The Smooth Delegator. Your work is now their work… wait, reverse that.
🐬 Mostly C's → The Dazzling Dolphin — The Social Superstar. Office morale MVP!
🦉 Mostly D's → The Watchful Owl — The Silent Strategist. They know everything. Everything. 👀
🐱 Mostly E's → The Cozy Cat — The Chill Survivor. Unbothered. Moisturized. In their lane.
